Our Story

Built in Geelong, for Geelong

Spark & Wire Electrical started as a one-van operation working out of Geelong, taking on rewires and repairs for homeowners who were sick of waiting weeks for a sparky to call back. Fifteen years on, we're still family-owned, still local, and Dave still picks up the phone himself.

What's changed is the scope of the work. Alongside residential rewiring, we now handle switchboard upgrades, solar panel installations, EV charger setups, LED lighting upgrades, smart home wiring and safety compliance work — for homeowners, builders and property managers across Geelong and the Surf Coast.

What hasn't changed is how we quote. Every job gets a fixed price before we start — no surprise call-out fees, no "it depends on the day" pricing. And if something urgent comes up, we offer same-day emergency service, because a dead switchboard doesn't wait for business hours.
