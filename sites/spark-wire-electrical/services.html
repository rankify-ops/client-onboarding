Fixed-price, licensed, and backed by 15 years on the tools. Here's what we do and how it works.

Residential Rewiring

Sleep easy knowing your wiring is safe

Older homes across Sunnybank, Holland Park and Carindale often still run on original wiring from decades ago — cloth insulation, undersized circuits, no safety switches. That's a fire risk hiding behind your walls, and it's the single job we get called out for most.

We handle full and partial rewires with minimal disruption: planning circuit-by-circuit, working room-by-room, and patching up after ourselves. Every rewire is tested and certified before we consider the job done.

Switchboard Upgrades

Stop resetting the same breaker every week

If your switchboard is still a ceramic fuse box, it's not just inconvenient — it's not protecting you the way a modern board does. We upgrade to a fully switch-protected board sized correctly for your home's actual load, including air conditioning, EV chargers, and pool equipment where needed.

Most switchboard upgrades in Sunnybank and Mount Gravatt homes are completed in a single day, with power restored before we leave.

Get a Quote for a Switchboard Upgrade
Smoke Alarm Compliance

Meet Queensland's smoke alarm laws without the guesswork

Queensland legislation requires interconnected, photoelectric smoke alarms in every bedroom, hallway and level of the home — and non-compliance is a real risk if you're selling, leasing or renewing insurance.

We assess your current setup, install or upgrade to fully compliant interconnected alarms, and give you paperwork confirming the work for your records or your agent.

Get a Quote for Smoke Alarms
EV Charger Installation

Charge at home, without overloading your board

A dryer outlet isn't an EV charger. We install dedicated home EV charging circuits sized to your vehicle and your switchboard's real capacity — including an upgrade first if your board can't handle it safely.

We've fitted EV chargers for households across Carindale and Wishart preparing for their first electric vehicle, and for homes future-proofing for a second one.

Cut your power bill and lift every room
