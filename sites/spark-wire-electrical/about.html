Our story

Started on a promise: turn up, quote straight, do it properly

Spark & Wire Electrical began 15 years ago as a one-van operation covering Sunnybank and the surrounding suburbs. Jake Mitchell had spent years watching homeowners get burned by tradies who didn't show up, quoted low then padded the invoice, or did just enough to pass and not a bit more.

Today Spark & Wire is still family-owned and still local — but now covers Sunnybank, Mount Gravatt, Holland Park, Carindale, Wishart and Upper Mount Gravatt, handling everything from full home rewires and switchboard upgrades to EV charger installs and smoke alarm compliance work.

What hasn't changed: every job is fixed-price before we start, every electrician on the tools is fully licensed, and every property is fully insured while we're on it. Safety first, every time — that's not a slogan, it's how we were trained.
