Our Story

Trusted Local Electricians Since Day One

Spark & Wire Electrical is a family-owned electrical business serving Geelong and the Surf Coast for over 15 years. We specialise in residential rewiring, switchboard upgrades, solar panel installations, and EV charger setups.

What sets us apart is our same-day emergency service and upfront fixed pricing — no hidden call-out fees. We work with homeowners, builders, and property managers across the Bellarine Peninsula, and we're proud to hold a 4.9 star rating from more than 180 Google reviews.
