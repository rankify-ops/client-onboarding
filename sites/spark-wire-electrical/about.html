Our Story

Premium tradie work, without the premium runaround

Spark & Wire Electrical is a family-owned electrical business that has served Geelong and the Surf Coast for over 15 years. We specialise in residential rewiring, switchboard upgrades, solar panel installations, and EV charger setups.

What sets us apart is our same-day emergency service and upfront fixed pricing — no hidden call-out fees. We work with homeowners, builders, and property managers right across the Bellarine Peninsula, from Geelong to Torquay and everywhere in between.

We built this business on a simple idea: a "premium tradie" feel doesn't mean premium prices, it means turning up on time, doing the job properly, and standing behind our work.
