FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before booking a Spark & Wire electrician.

Do you offer emergency electrical services?

Yes. We provide same-day emergency electrical repairs across Geelong and the Surf Coast — for outages, sparking outlets or tripped safety switches — with no hidden call-out fees.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, Spark & Wire Electrical is a fully licensed electrical contractor (REC 12345) and fully insured for every job, big or small.

How much does a switchboard upgrade cost?

Every switchboard is different, so we inspect your setup first and give you a fixed, upfront price before any work begins. No hidden extras.

Do you install solar panels and EV chargers?

Yes — we install solar panel systems sized to your property's usage, as well as home and commercial EV chargers wired safely to your switchboard.

What areas do you service?

We service Geelong, Geelong West, Newtown, Belmont, Highton, Ocean Grove, Barwon Heads, Torquay, Lara, Corio and the wider Bellarine Peninsula. See our full service area list.

Do you provide free quotes?

Yes, every quote is free and comes with fixed, upfront pricing so you know exactly what the job will cost before we start.

How quickly can you respond to an emergency?

We offer same-day response for electrical emergencies across our service area — call us directly for the fastest response.

Do you provide safety inspection and compliance certificates?

Yes, we carry out electrical safety inspections and provide compliance certificates for homeowners, landlords and property managers.

Do you work with builders and property managers, or just homeowners?

All three. We work with homeowners, builders and property managers across the Bellarine Peninsula, from single repairs to full new-build wiring.

Still have a question?

Give us a call or send us a message — we're happy to help.
